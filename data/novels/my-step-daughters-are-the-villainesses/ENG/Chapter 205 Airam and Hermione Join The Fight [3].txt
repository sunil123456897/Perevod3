"No!"

Hermione screamed.

Her voice tore through the clearing.

The Rank 2 Formula Ring above her hands flashed.

Until then, she had held the spell back. She had intended to release it carefully, directing the magic through narrow channels so it would strike only the enemy’s openings and avoid the Rubenhart knights.

Grief and rage broke that restraint.

Mana surged through the rings.

The air in front of her compressed.

Hermione thrust both hands forward.

The spell released.

A broad wave of force struck the Valmorian soldier who had killed the Rubenhart knight. It did not break his armor because of the Anti-mana, but it carried far more power than Hermione’s earlier support spells. The impact caught him in the chest and drove him backward.

His boots tore through the mud.

He crashed against the side of a supply wagon, splintering wood beneath his armored back.

The wagon shifted.

A crate fell from its edge and broke apart beside him.

The Valmorian knight remained conscious.

The serum kept him moving.

He pushed himself away from the wagon, one hand gripping his sword, and turned toward the last two wounded Rubenhart defenders.

Hermione stared at the dead knight on the grass.

His face had gone still.

Only a short time earlier, he had stood near the campfire with the others. He had smiled when Esther danced. He had laughed when Hermione tried to imitate Airam’s steps. Now he lay in the mud with blood beneath his neck, and there was nothing Hermione could do for him.

Her hands shook.

Then they closed.

Pale blue mana gathered around her fingers again.

The Valmorian soldier who had been thrown into the supply wagon started toward the wounded knight with the broken shield. The knight tried to stand, but her injured shoulder would not support her. She reached for a fallen sword instead.

The second Valmorian knight rushed toward Hermione and Airam.

He had seen the larger spell.

He understood that Hermione had become the greater threat.

His armor clattered as he crossed the grass. The serum had made him faster than any ordinary knight. He covered the distance with frightening speed, sword raised toward Hermione’s neck.

Airam stepped forward.

Her raised hand trembled.

Crimson runes appeared around her fingers.

They came too quickly to be safe.

Hermione saw them forming and knew at once that Airam had abandoned control. The runes did not drift separately as they had before. They gathered into a destructive cluster, pressing against one another in unstable shapes. Earth, pressure, binding, and sharp movement were forced together without the stabilizing order of a Formula Ring.

Witchcraft.

"Airam, wait—"

Airam closed her hand.

The runes exploded.

Crimson light burst across the clearing.

The Valmorian knight had no choice but to defend. He raised his anti-magic sword and threw himself backward as jagged pieces of earth erupted from the ground around him. Stone fragments shot upward like spears. Violent air pressure followed, smashing into his armor and hurling mud, grass, and broken pieces of wood through the air.
