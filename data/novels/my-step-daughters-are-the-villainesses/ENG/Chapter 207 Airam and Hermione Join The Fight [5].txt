Airam swayed.

Hermione caught her before she could fall into the mud.

The last of the black runes faded from Airam’s fingers, leaving dark marks beneath the skin of her hands and wrists. Her breathing was slightly broken. The destructive witchcraft had taken more from her than she wanted anyone to see, and it took a lot from her to pull back from that urge.

Hermione held her upright.

"You are hurt," she whispered.

"I am standing."

"That is not an answer."

Airam did not reply.

Across the clearing, Hendrick pushed himself up from the mud.

Blood covered one side of his face. His armor had been cut open in several places, and he moved with the stiffness of someone whose body had been pushed far beyond its limits. Yet he was alive.

Rina lay a short distance away.

Her shoulder was badly injured, and the right side of her armor had cracked beneath the force of the earlier attacks. She breathed through clenched teeth as she turned her head toward the two fallen Valmorian soldiers.

Neither moved.

Their black armor had been pierced and broken by Hermione’s storm of stones and weapons. The grass around them was torn apart. A spear remained buried in the ground near one body, its shaft trembling faintly from the force of its impact.

Hendrick looked over the battlefield.

Then he turned toward the sisters.

His eyes moved across Airam’s pale face, Hermione’s bloodied hands, and Esther clinging to them both. For a moment, relief appeared in his expression.

Esther ran forward.

She reached Airam and Hermione first, throwing her arms around both sisters.

"You are alive!" She cried. "You are both alive! I was so scared!"

Airam placed one tired hand over Esther’s hair.

Hermione closed her eyes briefly and held her younger sister close.

Hendrick began walking toward them.

A faint smile appeared on his face.

He opened his mouth, perhaps to tell them that they had done well, perhaps to order them into the tent where they would be safer, perhaps simply to reassure Esther that the immediate danger had passed.

Then his expression changed.

His gaze moved beyond them.

Rina looked in the same direction.

Airam and Hermione followed.

Even Esther turned.

Across the clearing, near the broken remains of Ulrich’s crimson barrier, Ulrich faced ten Valmorian knights.

They were moving toward him.

The soldiers had formed a wide arc around him, black armor reflecting the flames of the burning tree and supply wagon. Their weapons were drawn. Several carried anti-magic swords. Others held heavy spears or broad-bladed weapons built for close combat.

Mana leaked from beneath their armor in dark, unstable trails. Their bodies moved with unnatural speed. Their steps were heavy enough to tear through the grass, yet they crossed the ground with the coordination of trained soldiers rather than beasts.

Ulrich stood alone before them.

His coat had been torn at the side. Blood ran from the wound beneath his ribs and darkened the fabric at his waist. His sword remained in his hand, crimson runes flickering around the blade. The reinforcement spell was weaker than before. The light appeared and vanished in uneven pulses, repeatedly broken by the black swords that had struck it during his duel with Valdris.

Valdris watched from behind his knights.

His face had healed, but his expression remained cold.

The commander had stepped away from the immediate battle. He did not need to move while ten serum-enhanced soldiers stood between him and Ulrich.

The first Valmorian knight rushed forward.

He crossed the distance in an instant.

Ulrich moved.

The enemy’s sword came down toward his shoulder. Ulrich stepped outside the strike and turned his blade, guiding the black weapon past him. He cut toward the knight’s neck.

The sword struck armor.

Crimson mana shattered against the black metal.

Ulrich’s blade scraped along the helmet and shoulder guard without leaving more than a shallow mark.

The Valmorian knight turned immediately.

His armored elbow came toward Ulrich’s face.

Ulrich leaned back.

The blow passed close enough for the edge of the helmet to graze his cheek. The knight followed with a kick aimed at Ulrich’s knee.
