"Enough. I offered you a peaceful return. If you refuse to come willingly, I will take you by force."

Ulrich watched Valdris facing the sisters.

He moved immediately.

The four remaining Valmorian knights reacted before he could take more than two steps. They rushed him from different sides, their serum-enhanced bodies crossing the trampled grass with terrifying speed.

Ulrich raised his sword.

The first black blade struck his crimson reinforcement spell.

Red light shattered.

The second knight attacked from the right. Ulrich turned his sword and redirected the strike, but the third soldier came in immediately after. His blade struck Ulrich’s guard and drove him backward.

The fourth knight aimed for Ulrich’s wounded side.

Ulrich jumped away.

Pain tore through his ribs as he landed badly near the stream. His boots slipped in the wet grass. He caught himself before falling, but blood rose in his throat.

He spat it onto the ground.

The world swayed for a moment.

Smoke, firelight, and crimson mana blurred together before his eyes.

"My ladies," Hendrick shouted from near the tent. "Stay back!"

Hendrick moved immediately in front of the sisters.

Rina joined him.

Both had swallowed healing vials after Hermione’s earlier spell, but the potions had only done enough to keep them standing. Hendrick’s armor was split across his shoulder and ribs. Blood still darkened the edges of the broken plates. Rina held her sword in both hands, though one shoulder had been badly injured and her grip trembled from exhaustion.

They formed a line before Airam, Hermione, and Esther.

It would not be enough against Valdris.

Ulrich knew it.

If the Valdris chose to move now, Hendrick and Rina would die quickly. They had fought too long, bled too much, and spent too much mana. Their healing potions had closed some wounds, but no vial could return the strength lost through battle.

Ulrich’s expression turned more and more annoyed.

The four Valmorian knights circled him again.

His hand moved toward his chest.

’Do I have to use it?’

The Hollow Core.

The thought remained in his mind like a blade pressed against his throat.

It was not a tool he could use lightly.

The Hollow Core held the power to turn the situation around.

He also knew the cost.

The power did not obey him perfectly. It poured through him with more force than his body could safely handle. Every use left him weakened, drained, and unable to judge how much control he would retain once it awakened. If he called on it now, he might become strong enough to kill Valdris.

Or he might collapse before the man fell.

There were still four serum-enhanced knights around him.

If Ulrich could kill them first, he could create a brief opening. Then he could use the Hollow Core against Valdris alone. The plan was dangerous, but it gave him the best chance of ending the battle before the sisters were taken.

The reason he didn’t use it from the beginning was because of the consequences following right after. He wanted to use it at least when he killed half of their forces, but it looked like he had no choice now.

A black blade came toward his neck.

Ulrich ducked.

He rose with a cut toward the soldier’s throat, but the knight turned his helmet in time. Ulrich’s blade struck armor and scraped away.

Then Valdris spoke.

"Move back."

The four Valmorian knights obeyed instantly.

They jumped away from Ulrich, placing several paces of distance between themselves and the wounded Count.

Ulrich’s eyes narrowed.

Valdris raised one hand.

Mana gathered around his fingers.

The air above Ulrich changed.

A large Formula Ring appeared in the sky.

It formed so high above the clearing that its outer edge reached beyond the trees. Four concentric circles rotated through the smoke, bright blue against the night. Runes ignited along the rings in rapid succession. There were too many to count in a single glance. Lightning-unity formed the core of the spell, followed by layers of shaping, targeting, acceleration, and destructive force.

An Advanced spell.

Rank 4.

Ulrich’s eyes widened.

Valdris had prepared it while Ulrich fought the ten knights.

He had not been standing idle behind his soldiers. Every second Ulrich spent defending himself had given Valdris time to weave the larger formation. He had built the spell slowly, hidden among the smoke, fire mana, and chaos of the battlefield, waiting until Ulrich had exhausted himself enough that escape became difficult.
