"Lord Hendrick! Rina!"

Esther screamed, immediately watching them rush toward death.

Her voice broke as she reached out toward them.

Tears spilled down her cheeks again, and she tried to run forward, but Airam caught her around the waist and pulled her back.

"No," Airam said.

Esther struggled against her.

"E—Eldest sister! T—They are going to die!"

"You cannot go there!"

"He helped us!"

"I know."

Esther collapsed against Airam’s arms, crying hard enough that her whole body shook.

Across the clearing, Hendrick met the first Valmorian knight.

Their swords clashed.

The impact made Hendrick’s arms jerk downward. His reinforcement spell was gone. He had spent too much mana maintaining it during the earlier fight, and he no longer had time to rebuild the formation.

The Valmorian soldier still carried the strength of the serum.

He struck again.

Hendrick blocked.

Rina moved toward the second enemy, trying to keep him from reaching Hendrick’s side. Her sword met the black blade once, twice, three times. Each impact forced her farther back. Her injured shoulder could barely hold the weapon up.

The second Valmorian knight kicked at her wounded side.

She tried to move away.

The kick caught her anyway.

She was thrown into the mud, but she rolled before the enemy could bring his sword down. The black blade cut through the grass where her neck had been.

Hendrick saw her fall.

He tried to reach her.

The Valmorian knight before him did not allow it.

A spearhead came from the side.

Hendrick twisted away, but the point scraped across his ribs and tore through his armor. Blood spilled down his side. He answered with a slash toward the enemy’s neck, but the black armor turned the blade at the last instant.

The two Valmorian soldiers were pressing them apart.

Hendrick had to defend himself from one.

Rina had to survive the other.

Neither had enough strength left to win.

Hermione watched them struggle.

Her chest tightened.

She knew what would happen if she did nothing.

The same thing that had happened to the others.

Another Rubenhart knight would fall. Then Hendrick. Then the Valmorian soldiers would come for Airam, Esther, and her.

Hermione turned toward Airam.

"Airam."

Airam was still holding Esther against her side.

"What?"

"I need your help."

Airam looked at Hermione’s hands.

The tremors had not stopped. Faint burns of mana still marked Hermione’s palms from forcing the last Rank 3 spell into existence too quickly.

"You cannot cast another spell," Airam said.

"I have to."

"You nearly lost control."

"I know."

"Brian Strelley warned us about this."

Hermione’s face tightened.

The Grandmaster’s voice returned to her memory.

He had spoken to the sisters after seeing the way their mana reacted during training. Their power was too great for their age. It did not flow calmly like ordinary mana. It surged. It pressed against the boundaries of every Formula Ring they formed. It wanted to expand, to escape, to become more destructive than the spell required.

Airam’s witchcraft was dangerous because of the runes she controlled directly.

Esther’s magic was unstable because her fear and emotions affected the flow of her mana.

Hermione’s power was dangerous for another reason.

When she focused, she could weave spells too quickly.

She could force too many runes into place before her body had time to understand the strain. Her Formula Rings would become larger and stronger than she intended. If she lost control, the spell might strike allies as easily as enemies.
