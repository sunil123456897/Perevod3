Ulrich looked at the sisters.

Then he turned back toward the eight Valmorian knights.

His sword rose.

Crimson mana gathered over the blade once more.

He rushed forward.

The eight Valmorian knights had barely recovered from Airam’s witchcraft when he reached them. Crimson mana clung to his sword in tightly bound layers, the reinforcement spell rebuilt once more despite the strain in his hand.

The nearest knight raised his anti-magic blade.

Ulrich did not meet it directly.

He stepped to the side, caught the attack near the lower edge of his sword, and guided it past his body. The black blade tore through the crimson reinforcement, scattering red sparks across the grass. Ulrich had already moved beyond the strike.

His sword came toward the knight’s neck.

The helmet turned in time.

Ulrich’s blade scraped along the black metal and cut across the gap beneath the ear guard. Blood appeared, though the wound was not deep enough.

A second knight attacked from behind.

Dark red runes flashed across the ground beneath the soldier’s boots.

Airam had raised her hand again.

The runes did not try to crush the knight or bind him completely. That would have required more force, more control, and more time than she could spare. Instead, they pulled at the ground beneath his feet. Mud gathered around his boots. Stones shifted beneath the grass. The knight’s next step landed poorly.

His sword missed Ulrich’s back.

Ulrich turned.

He drove the pommel of his weapon into the side of the soldier’s helmet, then cut toward the inside of the man’s elbow. The blade found the armor joint.

The Valmorian knight’s arm dropped.

Ulrich thrust toward the opening beneath the helmet.

A third soldier intercepted him.

Black steel struck crimson mana.

Ulrich’s spell shattered.

The impact forced his arm down, pain shooting through his wrist. He stepped backward, trying to create distance, but the Valmorian knights moved with him.

Their black armor really made them difficult to wound, and the serum allowed them to endure injuries that would have stopped most men. One had blood running from beneath his helmet; another held an injured arm close to his side. Neither retreated.

They closed around Ulrich again.

Hermione lifted her hands.

Small Formula Rings appeared around her wrists.

She did not aim the spells at the armor. She sent bursts of wind, force, and light toward the soldiers’ surroundings. A flash of pale light struck the narrow eye slit of one helmet. A sudden gust swept smoke and ash across another’s face. A force pulse struck a fallen sword near a third knight’s boots, sending the weapon skidding beneath his step.

None of the attacks could defeat them.

But each one made them look away.

Each one made them hesitate.

That was enough for Ulrich.

A knight turned his head to avoid Hermione’s light.

Ulrich stepped through the opening and cut beneath the man’s helmet. The blade entered the gap at the throat. The Valmorian soldier choked and staggered backward, clutching at the wound.

Ulrich pulled his sword free.

The knight fell.

Seven remained.

As much as the serum made them stronger, it wasn’t perfect, it seemed. It was like it was dumbing them a bit.

Airam’s runes flashed again.

A loose wind rune caught the shoulder of another enemy, turning him just enough that his sword struck the ground instead of Ulrich’s ribs. A binding rune wrapped briefly around the soldier’s ankle. The anti-magic weapon in his hand broke the effect at once, but Ulrich had already used the pause.

He jumped over the lowered blade.

His boot struck the knight’s shoulder plate as he passed. Ulrich twisted in the air and brought his sword down into the narrow seam between helmet and breastplate.

The sword entered.

The knight collapsed into the grass.

Six remained.

Ulrich landed heavily.

His side burned.

Blood had soaked through his coat now. His movements were still fast, but they had lost some of their earlier sharpness. Every time he turned too quickly, pain pulled through his ribs. Every time his sword met black steel, numbness climbed farther into his arm.

The Valmorian knights saw it.

They pressed forward harder.

One swung high.

Ulrich ducked beneath it.

Another attacked low.

Ulrich jumped back.

A third knight came from the side and drove a shoulder into Ulrich’s chest. The impact knocked the breath from him. He stumbled across the wet grass, barely keeping his sword raised before another blade came down toward his head.

Airam’s runes exploded against the ground between them.

The knight jumped back.

Ulrich used the space.

He formed a small air spell beneath his boots and shot sideways through the smoke. The sudden movement carried him out of the encirclement, but two Valmorian soldiers turned immediately and followed him.
