BOOOOM!!

A tower of flame exploded upward beneath him, swallowing Valdris in a roaring column of red and gold. The blast tore apart the grass around the stream. Stones shattered. Water flashed into steam. The heat struck the camp in a violent wave, forcing nearby knights to raise their arms over their faces.

The flame climbed high into the night.

For one second, it looked as though a crimson pillar had been driven from the earth toward the clouds.

Hermione cried out.

Airam pulled Esther close against her chest, turning the younger girl away from the explosion. Esther still tried to look over Airam’s arm, tears running down her cheeks as the light painted her face red.

Even the battle around the tents faltered.

Rubenhart knights and Valmorian soldiers alike looked toward the stream.

The pillar of fire burned bright enough to turn the trees into black silhouettes.

Then it collapsed.

Smoke poured across the clearing.

Ash drifted over the water.

A dark figure burst from the smoke.

Valdris landed hard on the far bank of the stream, one knee striking the ground. The remains of his black coat flew from his shoulders in scorched strips. Fire clung to part of the fabric before dying in the wet grass.

He rose slowly.

The left side of his face had been burned.

Red marks covered his cheek and jaw, reaching toward his ear. His hair had been singed near the temple. The right sleeve of his coat had burned away from elbow to wrist, exposing skin marked with dark burns beneath the blood from Ulrich’s earlier cut.

Valdris looked at Ulrich.

His expression was twisted into a furious and ugly grimace.

The calm was entirely gone.

So was the confidence he had carried into the camp.

"How?"

Ulrich said nothing.

"How did you hide a spell of that rank from me?"

His voice rose slightly.

The question was not one of simple curiosity. Valdris needed to understand. The Formula Ring beneath him had held too many runes to be formed without preparation. It had required careful placement, proper geometry, and enough mana to remain stable until release. A spell of that size should have produced a clear magical presence.

He should have felt it.

The anti-magic blade should have reacted to it.

Yet it had remained beneath his feet until Ulrich chose to trigger it.

Ulrich’s mouth moved.

"The heat from your cinder cloud," he said. "The steam from my water spell. The pressure from your enhancement aura."

Valdris’s eyes narrowed.

Ulrich continued.

"You filled the clearing with fire remnants. I let the remaining traces settle into the ground. The spell’s outer ring copied the same pattern. Its mana signature blended with yours."

Valdris did not speak.
